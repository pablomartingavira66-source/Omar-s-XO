Field manual

Tactical guide

Ultimate XO is tic-tac-toe on another level: nine small boards inside one big board, and every move decides where your opponent must play. Learn the rules in two minutes.

⏱ 2 min to learn 🎮 6 game modes 🏆 10 league divisions
The rules

Six rules, that's all

Understand rule 2 and you understand the whole game.

1

A board inside a board

The field is nine small boards, each with nine squares. You are X, your opponent is O.

2

Your move picks his board

The square you play (say top-right) forces your opponent to play in the matching small board (the top-right one).

3

Win a small board

Three in a row inside a small board (across, down or diagonal) and that board is yours.

4

Win the match

Win three small boards in a row on the big board and the match is yours.

5

Closed board = free move

If you are sent to a board that is already won or full, you may play in any open board (they glow yellow).

6

Draws

A board that fills up with no winner is closed and counts for nobody. If nothing is left to play and nobody has a line, the match is a draw.

Mystery box

Six box effects

A box appears every 5 moves (the counter is above the board). Opening one doesn't cost a move unless the effect ends your turn.

VIRUS 20%Steal any open small board: it becomes yours instantly. The turn then passes.
GRAVITY 20%Marks float for a moment. A visual surprise, the rules stay the same.
SWAP 20%Pick any two squares and swap what is in them. Build your line, break his, even win a board.
LOCKDOWN 20%The board where you opened the box locks for 10 seconds, nobody can play in it, and you continue anywhere else.
PURGED 10%That small board is wiped clean (even if someone had won it) and the turn passes.
TRAP 10%Your opponent's mark appears in that square and you lose your turn.
💡 Tip: a box cuts both ways. Open it when you need a swing, and leave it alone when you are clearly ahead.

Pro tips

Think like a champion

  1. Think about where you send your opponent, not just where you play. The best move often sends him to a board where he has nothing.
  2. Never send him to a board he is about to win. If he has two in a row there, you just handed him the keys.
  3. Winning a board isn't always good. If your winning square sends him to a closed board, he gets a free move.
  4. The centre and corner boards sit in more lines on the big board. Fight for them early.
  5. A free move is a weapon. When you get one, finish a line or cut one of his before it lands.
  6. Read the commentary. The game flags blunders and brilliant moves. Learn from them.
  7. In Nomad, build two threats at once. He can only block one.
FAQ

Still wondering?

Why can I play in any board?

Because the board you were meant to play in is already won or full. You get a free move, and the open boards glow yellow.

Can I play offline?

If you installed the app, Local, VS AI, Mystery and Nomad work without internet. Online matches, the global leaderboard and cloud saving need a connection.

How is my progress saved?

Signed in, it saves automatically and follows you to any device. As a guest, it stays on this device only.

How do I rematch online?

When the game ends, tap Rematch. Once your friend accepts, a fresh board starts in the same room.

What happens when the shot clock runs out?

The game plays a random move for you, so watch the bar turn red.

Do two-player local games count as wins?

No. There is no single "you" to credit, so they are recorded as "played". Wins and losses come from VS AI, Online and the League.
